Yuze Zhai 翟宇泽

Hi there! I am a 4th-year undergraduate student majoring in Robotics at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), with research interests in humanoid robotics and robot learning. During my junior year, I was a visiting student at UCSD, working on humanoid robots. I am applying for PhD positions starting Fall 2027.

I am particularly interested in whole-body loco-manipulation, reinforcement learning, and sim-to-real. My research goal is to build general-purpose robots.

I am passionate about the intersection of Robotics, Computer Vision and Reinforcement Learning. Feel free to reach out via email if you are interested in my research or potential collaborations :)
